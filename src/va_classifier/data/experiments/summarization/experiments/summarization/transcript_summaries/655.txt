In a candid conversation, Ellie, originally from Detroit, discusses her life experiences, including her military service, the challenges of motherhood, and her aspirations to own an educational facility. She shares her struggles with PTSD stemming from her military experience, including a traumatic incident of rape, and the emotional toll it has taken on her, such as mood swings and sleep difficulties. Ellie expresses her commitment to therapy, particularly group sessions at the veterans hospital, which help her feel less isolated in her struggles. Despite her challenges, she takes pride in her role as a dedicated mother and grandmother, and she reflects on the importance of family and personal growth.
