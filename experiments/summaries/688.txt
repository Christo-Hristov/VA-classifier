In a conversation, Ellie, a military brat from Texas, shares her struggles with fatigue, trust issues, and the impact of her past drug addiction on her life. She reflects on her childhood in Japan and her time in the military, expressing regret over lost relationships and the challenges of transitioning to civilian life. Despite having been diagnosed with PTSD and depression, she is currently in therapy and sees a psychiatrist regularly. Ellie discusses her feelings of low energy and motivation, her coping mechanisms, and her pride in overcoming her drug addiction. She also reminisces about her love for reading, which she has struggled to maintain, and expresses a desire to reconnect with positive influences in her life.
